TV Glow

Syncs your TV lights to whatever is on screen. For Android TV and Google TV.

Download TV Glow

The download starts by itself in a few seconds.

  1. On your phone: Govee Home app → your light → gear icon → turn on LAN Control.
  2. When the download finishes, choose Install. If the TV asks, allow Downloader to install apps.
  3. Open TV Glow. Setup walks you through the rest with the remote.

Works with Govee Wi-Fi lights that have LAN Control, and with WLED. Apps running on the TV sync (YouTube, Plex, games); protected apps like Netflix and HDMI inputs like consoles can't be captured.
